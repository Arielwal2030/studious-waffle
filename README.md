# SeatSmart

A free, private seating chart planner for weddings and events. It runs entirely in the browser, with no sign-up, no server and no tracking.

- Add guests one at a time or paste a whole list (`Name, Party`)
- **Auto-seat** keeps parties and families together and never seats "keep apart" pairs at the same table
- Drag and drop on desktop, or tap a guest and then tap a table on a phone
- Warnings for overfull tables, seating conflicts and split families
- Print a clean chart, export CSV (for place cards and caterers), or save and open plan files
- Saves automatically in the browser

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. There's nothing to install.

## Host it free on GitHub Pages

1. On GitHub, go to **Settings → Pages**
2. Under **Source**, pick **Deploy from a branch**, then choose `main` and `/ (root)`
3. Your app goes live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/studious-waffle/`

## Making money from it (honestly)

Nothing guarantees income. What follows is a realistic plan, roughly in order of effort:

1. **Tips.** Set `SUPPORT_URL` at the top of `app.js` to a free Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee page. A link then appears in the footer.
2. **Get found.** Post it where couples plan weddings, such as r/weddingplanning, wedding Facebook groups and Pinterest. Lead with "free, no sign-up." That's what sets it apart from the paid tools.
3. **Sell printables.** Sell matching seating chart signs, escort cards and place cards as Canva templates on Etsy, and link them from the app. The free app brings in visitors and the printables bring in the money.
4. **Affiliate links.** Add links to place-card and table-number products through Amazon Associates or Etsy affiliates.
5. **Pro version** (later, if people use it): a paid tier on Gumroad with a round and long table floor-plan view, meal-choice tracking, and sharing with a partner or planner.
6. **Other events.** The same tool works for galas, bar and bat mitzvahs, corporate dinners and classroom seating. Each one is a new landing page and a new audience.
