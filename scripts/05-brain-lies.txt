title: Your Brain Is Lying To You
tags: overthinking, anxiety, mindset, negativity bias, self improvement
---
Your brain is not trying to make you happy.
It's trying to keep you alive.
That's why one criticism sticks longer than ten compliments.
Psychologists call it the negativity bias.
Your mind treats a bad moment like a threat, and replays it so you'll be ready next time.
Useful when you're dodging predators. Not so useful in a meeting.
So the next time your thoughts spiral, ask one question.
Is this true, or is this just loud?
Most of the time, it's just loud.
You don't have to believe every thought you think.
Follow for one mindset shift a day.
