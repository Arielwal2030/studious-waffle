title: Why Comparing Yourself Is Rigged
tags: comparison, self worth, mindset, social media, self improvement
---
Comparing yourself to others is a rigged game.
You're comparing your behind the scenes to everyone else's highlight reel.
You see their promotion. You don't see the years of rejection.
You see their body. You don't see the struggle behind it.
You see their smile. You don't see their bad nights.
You're judging your full story against one chapter of theirs.
There's only one fair comparison.
You, today, against you, last year.
Are you a little wiser? A little stronger? A little kinder?
That's the only race that counts.
And you're the only one running it.
Follow for one mindset shift a day.
