title: Stop Waiting For Motivation
tags: motivation, discipline, mindset, habits, self improvement
---
Motivation is a terrible boss.
It shows up late, leaves early, and never when you need it.
People who get things done aren't more motivated than you.
They just stopped asking how they feel about it.
They built a routine that runs without permission.
Same time. Same place. Same first step.
Action creates motivation, not the other way around.
You don't wait to feel like running. You run, and then you feel like a runner.
So stop waiting for the feeling.
Decide when, decide where, and show up.
The feeling will catch up with you.
Follow for one mindset shift a day.
