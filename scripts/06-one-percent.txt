title: The Power of 1% Better
tags: habits, growth, consistency, mindset, self improvement
---
Want to change your life? Aim for one percent.
Not a whole new you. Just one percent better than yesterday.
Here's the math.
Get one percent better every day for a year, and you end up about thirty seven times better.
Small gains don't look like much on any single day.
That's why most people quit.
They expect the results to show up as fast as the effort.
But growth is quiet before it gets loud.
Read one page. Walk ten minutes. Save one dollar.
Tiny on day one. Massive by day three hundred sixty five.
Follow for one mindset shift a day.
