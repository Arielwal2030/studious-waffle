title: Stop Checking Your Phone First Thing
tags: morning routine, phone, focus, mindset, productivity
---
What's the first thing you do when you wake up?
If it's grabbing your phone, you're starting your day on someone else's agenda.
Emails. News. Notifications. Other people's lives.
Before you've had a single thought of your own, you're already reacting.
Try this for one week.
Give yourself the first twenty minutes.
Drink water. Stretch. Write down one thing that matters today.
The phone will still be there.
But you'll meet it as someone with a plan, not someone being pulled around.
How you start the day is how you live the day.
Follow for one mindset shift a day.
