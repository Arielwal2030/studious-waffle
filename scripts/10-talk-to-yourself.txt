title: Talk to Yourself Like a Friend
tags: self talk, self compassion, confidence, mindset, self improvement
---
Would you talk to your best friend the way you talk to yourself?
You're so stupid. You always mess up. You'll never get it right.
You'd never say that to someone you love.
So why say it to yourself?
The voice in your head shapes how you act.
Harsh self talk doesn't make you better. It makes you afraid to try.
Next time you mess up, pause.
Ask what you'd tell a friend in the same spot.
Probably something like, that was hard, you'll get it next time.
Say that to yourself instead.
You're going to be with you for the rest of your life. Be on your own side.
Follow for one mindset shift a day.
