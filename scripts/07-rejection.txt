title: Rejection Is Just Data
tags: rejection, resilience, failure, mindset, self improvement
---
Rejection feels personal. It almost never is.
A no usually means not this, not now, or not them.
It doesn't mean not you, not ever.
Try treating every rejection like data.
What can you learn? What would you change? Who do you ask next?
The people who succeed don't get rejected less.
They get rejected more, because they keep asking.
Every no gets you closer to the yes that changes everything.
So collect your nos like proof that you're trying.
Most people never collect any.
Follow for one mindset shift a day.
