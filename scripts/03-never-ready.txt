title: You Will Never Feel Ready
tags: confidence, fear, mindset, courage, self improvement
---
Here's something nobody tells you.
You will never feel ready.
Not for the new job. Not for the hard conversation. Not for the big move.
Ready is not a feeling you get before you start.
It's a skill you build after you start.
Every confident person you admire was once terrified, doing it anyway.
They didn't wait for the fear to leave.
They just let it come along for the ride.
So if you're waiting until you feel ready, you're waiting for something that isn't coming.
Start scared. Start messy. Just start.
Ready shows up halfway through.
Follow for one mindset shift a day.
