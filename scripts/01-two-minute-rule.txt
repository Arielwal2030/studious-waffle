title: The 2-Minute Rule That Kills Procrastination
tags: procrastination, productivity, mindset, habits, self improvement
---
You're not lazy. You're starting too big.
Here's the fix. It's called the two-minute rule.
Whatever you're avoiding, shrink it until it takes two minutes.
Don't go to the gym. Just put your shoes on.
Don't write the essay. Just write one sentence.
Don't clean the house. Just clear one surface.
Sounds too small to matter, right?
That's exactly why it works.
Your brain resists big, vague tasks. It doesn't resist tiny ones.
And once you've started, keeping going is easy.
The hardest part was never the work. It was the first step.
So make the first step so small you can't say no.
Follow for one mindset shift a day.
